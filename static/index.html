    Install once. Play forever.

    The AI runs on your own Mac, so a small helper does the work in the background. Install it once and this website uses it from then on.

    1. Open Terminal
      (Applications → Utilities).
    2. Paste this and press Return:
    3. Wait 5–10 minutes while it downloads (~1 GB). This page connects by itself.
    Looking for the helper on this Mac…

    Needs a Mac (Apple Silicon recommended — Intel works but is slow), about 3 GB of free space and Homebrew (the installer tells you if it's missing).

    Already installed? Open Backing Track Studio Helper from Applications. If your browser asks whether this site may access devices on your local network, choose Allow. If Safari won't connect, try Chrome.

    Chord chart Edited by you

    ·
    Click a bar and type its chords as you'd write them: Gm7 fills the bar, Gm7 C7 splits it in half, four chords give one per beat, % repeats the bar before, NC means no chord. Enter saves · Tab next bar · Esc cancels.

    Chords are detected automatically — fix any that sound wrong with Edit chords (or double-click a chord in the lane). Your changes are saved with the song. Click a bar to jump there · % = same as the bar before.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    ⌘KJump to a song or run any command
    SpacePlay / pause
    RStart / stop recording yourself
    ← →Back / forward one bar (or 5 s if no beat was found); with Shift: 4 bars
    Home or 0Jump to start (or start of the loop)
    1–7Mute / unmute a part (top to bottom) · with Shift: solo
    LLoop on / off
    C · KCount-in on / off · metronome click on / off
    [ ]Slower / faster (5% steps)
    - =Key down / up a semitone
    V · FShow / hide the video · video fullscreen (drag the bar under the video to resize it)
    AA/B: compare with the original song
    H · GChord chart (Edit chords there to fix them; or double-click a chord in the lane) · show / hide the chords
    NShow / hide the notes the sax / brass plays (written for your instrument; click one to jump there)
    TTuner — shows the note written for your instrument, and how many cents sharp or flat
    EExport…
    M · , .Add a marker · jump to the previous / next marker or chorus
    Z · Shift+ZZoom in (to the loop, if there is one) · show the whole song
    MouseClick a waveform or the bar ruler to jump · drag to select a section (snaps to beats; hold Alt for free selection) · drag a section's edge to adjust it · double-click a slider to reset it · double-click a take's name to rename it · pinch or ⌘-scroll to zoom, scroll sideways to move · double-click the ruler to add a marker; Shift-click a marker to loop that section · double-click the song title to rename it · click a chorus or section in the Form strip to loop it, Shift-click to add more

    Settings

    New songs open with your part already muted, and keys are written for your instrument.
    ms
    If your takes sound late, increase this; if early, decrease it. You can also nudge each take with ◀ ▶.

    Tip: wear headphones when recording, otherwise the backing track leaks into your microphone.
